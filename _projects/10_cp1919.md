[CP1919](https://cp1919.xyz) is an independent, fan-run archive of one image: the pulse-line graphic from the sleeve of Joy Division's 1979 album _Unknown Pleasures_.

The site follows the image from its origin as a plot of pulsar signals to the T-shirts, tattoos, trainers, and parodies it has turned into since. Finds are sorted into collections, there is a short history of the image, and anyone can submit a sighting.

It is a fan project and is not affiliated with Joy Division, Factory Records, or Peter Saville.
