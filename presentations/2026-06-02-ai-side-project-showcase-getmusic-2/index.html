5 Product-Led Growth Loops Inside GetMusic

How AI helps a solo founder operate built-in distribution without a team.

AI is not the growth strategy. AI makes the growth strategy operable by one person.
Context

What GetMusic does

  • Bandcamp artists have releases and codes they want people to discover.
  • Fans want music discovery, not just another feed.
  • GetMusic distributes, organizes, promotes, and recirculates independent music.
Frame

Product loop first. Automation second. AI third.

01

Product-led distribution

Uploads, profiles, social handles, spotlights, catalog pages, radio links, notifications.

02

Automation

Jobs, queues, schedules, Buffer / Make flows, review workflows.

03

AI ops

Summaries, platform copy, moderation, tag descriptions, playlist blurbs, featured drafts.

Not everything here is AI. That is the important part.

Loop 1

Uploads become distribution moments

Artist upload→Release page→Generated summary→Social posts→Future recirculation
The upload is not the end of the workflow. It is the start of the distribution loop.

Transferable lesson: design product actions so they create reusable assets.

Loop 2

Artist metadata becomes attribution and reach

  • Names
  • Links
  • Social handles
  • Genres
  • Locations
  • Release metadata
The profile data is not just profile data. It is attribution infrastructure.

AI fit: clean up descriptions, adapt copy per channel, generate short blurbs from structured metadata.

Loop 3

Notifications create re-share moments

Feature event→Artist notification→Social tag→Reshare
If the product creates a moment someone is proud of, tell them at the exact moment they are most likely to share it.
Loop 4

Spotlights turn artists into co-marketers

Input

Bio, origin story, creative process, release inspiration, fan-engagement details.

Output

A polished feature the artist helped create and now has a reason to share.

Transferable lesson: ask users for structured raw material, then turn it into something polished they want to distribute.

Loop 5

Distribution satellites extend the surface area

IndieCratesRadio pagesSonicSiftFeatured postsPopular releasesPlaylistsCatalog recirculation
A release I onboarded two years ago can still be posted today, in copy I never wrote.
AI ops layer

AI as the missing tiny team

AI-assisted job What it replaces
Release summaries Editorial intern
Platform-specific posts Social media manager
Tag / genre descriptions Copywriter
Playlist descriptions Curator / newsletter writer
Featured post drafts Blogger
Offensive-material checks Trust and safety reviewer
Closing lesson

The moat is the loop, not the LLM.

AI became useful because the product creates structured distribution opportunities.

If AI can help anyone build features faster, the defensible part is the loop around what the product does.
