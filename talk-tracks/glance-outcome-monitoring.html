Five-minute Glance demo

One story, end to end

Spend no more than 90 seconds on slides. The rest belongs in the product and the terminal.

"Glance tells you when automated work didn't happen and hands the fix to an AI agent."
0:00 to 0:45

Slide 1: the problem

Stay on the slide. Do not open the product yet.
"Quick poll: how do you know your nightly automation did its job last night?"

Pause. Let people answer. Then:

"I built Glance because I had 77 automations and no system of record for whether they actually did their jobs."

Say the one-liner. Then move straight into the real dashboard.

0:45 to 1:30

Live: the real dashboard

Use the dogfooding account with the real monitors. Do not tour the product.

Point at only three things:

  1. A group that is green.
  2. One passing check's outcome receipt: metric, observed value, threshold, and timestamp.
  3. The attention cards. Only work that needs a human belongs there.
"A green checkmark is not enough. Every green state carries the receipt for why it is green."
1:30 to 3:00

Live: break one outcome

Use the pre-staged demo check. Trigger the failure from the terminal with the rehearsed command.

Let the room watch the check go red. Open it and show:

  • The expectation that was violated.
  • The observed evidence and run history.
  • The remediation playbook and inputs an agent can use.
"Uptime monitoring asks whether the endpoint is up. Job monitoring asks whether the process ran. Glance asks whether the expected outcome happened."
"Glance never fires an alert it cannot explain."
3:00 to 4:15

Live: the agent closes the loop

Show the generated remediation work item. In the terminal, have the agent claim the next work item over the REST API.

Keep the explanation short while the agent works:

"The failure already carries the expectation, evidence, history, and playbook. The agent does not have to reconstruct the incident from a vague page."

When the check recovers, show the resolution receipt: the run that fixed it, the work item that was active, and the resolution note.

"The incident closed itself, and I can audit exactly why."

If the agent run is too slow or unpredictable, play the prepared 45-second capture. Do not improvise through a stalled terminal.

4:15 to 5:00

Slides 2 and 3: category and ask

On slide 2:

"Glance is outcome monitoring for automations plus a work queue AI agents claim over REST. It is not uptime monitoring, workflow automation, or agent orchestration. It does not do the work. It makes automated work accountable."

On slide 3:

"I am looking for operators with one automation whose failure actually costs them something. I will help you put it on Glance."

Point to glnc.io. Repeat the one-liner and stop.

Demo prep

Primary path

  • Real dogfooding account already open.
  • Green group expanded to the chosen receipt.
  • Demo check configured with a fast, reliable threshold.
  • Terminal commands in shell history.

Fallback path

  • Second tab already open on a red check.
  • 45-second agent remediation capture queued locally.
  • Static resolution receipt ready if the network fails.
  • Never debug on stage.

Q&A lines

How is this different from healthchecks.io? They verify that the run happened. Glance verifies that the expected outcome happened, and silence counts as failure.
Is this agent orchestration? No. Agents bring their own runtime. Glance is the accountable queue they claim work from.
What does it cost? Price the protected workflows, not alert or monitor volume. Use the current beta answer in the room.
